
# 📊 MEDGINI — Market Research & Data Analytics

### *From Field Research → Data → SQL → Power BI → Business Insights*

A hands-on **Market Research and Data Analytics project** completed during my internship with **Medgini**, covering the complete journey from collecting real-world market survey data to analysing it using **SQL, Excel, Power Query and Power BI**.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The project focused on understanding **consumer cough-syrup purchasing behaviour** through field-level market research conducted across **Ballia, Basti and Gorakhpur**.

The collected survey data was subsequently transformed into a structured analytics workflow using:

**Field Research → Data Preparation → SQL Analysis → Excel → Power Query → Power BI → Business Insights**

The objective was not only to collect data, but to turn raw market observations into **meaningful, business-oriented insights**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand **consumer purchasing behaviour** for cough-syrup products.
* Analyse **brand demand and preferences** across different locations.
* Identify differences in purchasing patterns across **customer categories**.
* Evaluate **outlet-level and city-level performance**.
* Study **product substitution and purchase behaviour**.
* Build an interactive dashboard to communicate the findings effectively.

---

## 📍 Market Research

The field research involved visiting medical stores and recording customer purchase information through structured surveys.

### Research Coverage

| Parameter           | Details                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 📍 Cities           | Ballia, Basti, Gorakhpur |
| 📝 Survey Records   | **660**                  |
| 🏪 Medical Outlets  | Multiple outlets         |
| 📦 Product Category | Cough Syrups             |
| 📅 Research Period  | June–July 2026           |

The survey captured information related to **requested brand, final brand, quantity, customer category, prescription status, purchase behaviour, substitution and other market-level attributes**.

---

## 🗄️ SQL Business Analytics

The collected dataset was analysed using **MySQL**, with a focus on answering practical business questions rather than only performing basic database operations.

The SQL analysis covered:

* Brand-wise demand and frequency
* **Market share analysis**
* City-wise brand performance
* Outlet-level performance
* Customer-category analysis
* Prescription vs. non-prescription behaviour
* Product substitution patterns
* Purchase quantity analysis
* Ranking and comparative analysis
* Aggregate and window-function based analysis

The repository contains the complete SQL query collection used for the analysis.

---

## 📗 Excel & Power Query

Before visualization, the survey dataset was prepared and explored using **Excel and Power Query**.

### Key learning areas

* Data cleaning and organization
* Data transformation
* Handling inconsistent/raw survey data
* Column and value standardization
* Data preparation for analysis

This stage helped convert the raw survey responses into a cleaner and more analysis-ready dataset.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

A fully interactive **Power BI dashboard** was developed to transform the analysed survey data into an easy-to-understand business report.

### Dashboard includes

* 📌 **Key Performance Indicators**
* 📈 Time-wise purchase trends
* 🏷️ Brand-wise demand analysis
* 📍 City-wise contribution
* 🏪 Outlet-level comparison
* 👥 Customer-category analysis
* 🔄 Product substitution analysis
* 🎛️ Interactive outlet filters
* 📊 Comparative tables and visualizations

The dashboard was built while gaining hands-on experience with **Power Query, data modelling, visualization techniques and interactive report design**.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

The analysis provides a clear view of how cough-syrup demand varies across **brands, locations, outlets and customer segments**.

Some of the dashboard-level observations include:

* **Corex** emerged as the highest-requested brand in the analysed dataset.
* **Gorakhpur** contributed the highest share of recorded purchase quantity among the three cities.
* Customer categories showed noticeable differences in brand demand and purchasing behaviour.
* Outlet-level analysis revealed significant variation in product quantities and brand preferences.
* Time-based analysis helped identify changes and fluctuations in purchase activity during the research period.
